## About Me

<div class="about-section">

  <div class="about-text">
    <p>I am a geospatial scientist with a Ph.D. in Spatial Informatics from IIIT Hyderabad, currently a post-doctoral Research Associate at the Indian Agricultural Research Institute (ICAR-IARI), New Delhi. For more than twelve years I have worked across the full chain of Earth observation – flying drones, collecting field spectra, processing satellite archives, building machine-learning and physical models, and shipping the software that puts the results in people's hands.</p>

    <p>My work spans crop health, soil health and soil moisture, reservoir water quality and coastal ecosystems. I have built six geospatial applications – among them a Google Earth Engine platform for near-real-time crop monitoring and <strong>DroneMapper</strong>, a desktop drone-mapping suite comparable to Pix4D – co-authored the book <em>Drone Imaging and Analytics for Agriculture</em> (ICAR-IARI, 2026), and published 24 peer-reviewed papers. I also enjoy teaching: I have taught university students for eight years and trained faculty, scientists and working professionals across India.</p>

## Core Competencies

* **Cloud & Web GIS:** Google Earth Engine app development and big data processing.
* **Satellite Data Processing:** Optical and SAR data processing.
* **UAV Analytics:** Processing multispectral, hyperspectral, and thermal data.
* **Spatial Algorithms:** Machine learning integration, physical modeling, and raster/vector processing.
