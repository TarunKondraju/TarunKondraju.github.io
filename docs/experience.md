## Products Developed

* **PUSAECMS:** A Google Earth Engine application for near-real-time crop health management using vegetation indices, water indices, engineered composite indices and physically based biophysical variable estimation. Users can perform quantitative analysis and visualize temporal changes in crop health at any location.
* **PUSA eCMS Desktop:** A multi-backend Python/PyQt6 desktop application that streams Sentinel-2 imagery from Microsoft Planetary Computer, Copernicus Data Space and Google Earth Engine and generates LAI, CCC and CWC maps with Gaussian process regression.
* **DroneMapper:** An open-source Windows desktop photogrammetry application (Python, PySide6), comparable to Pix4D, that builds point clouds, Digital Surface Models, Digital Terrain Models and multiband orthomosaics from RGB, multispectral and thermal UAV images.
* **VASUDHA:** An application to monitor soil health (N, P, K, OC, EC and pH) from VNIR/SWIR and MIR spectra generated by a spectroradiometer and FTIR, in real time or in offline mode for large volumes of spectral data.
* **PAUDHA:** An application to assess crop health from large amounts of spectroradiometer data in real-time or offline mode.
* **PUSAMIA / EdgeProcessing:** An automated edge-computing pipeline that co-registers and layer-stacks UAV multispectral images and maps crop urea requirement in real time.
